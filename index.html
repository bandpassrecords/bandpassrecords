About

A label that also writes software.

We started as a record label, putting out psytrance from artists we believe in.

Making music meant juggling projects across DAWs, calibrating headphones and cutting promo videos by hand, so we started writing software for it.

Lately the studio has gone beyond music, with apps like Negativo and Retro1.
